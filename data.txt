Description
-----------
The MIMIC-IV Demo is a publicly available subset of the full MIMIC-IV
database, containing de-identified electronic health records for
approximately 100 ICU patients admitted to Beth Israel Deaconess
Medical Center, Boston, MA.

It covers two modules:
  - hosp/   Hospital-level data (admissions, diagnoses, medications, labs)
  - icu/    ICU-level data (stays, chart events, procedures)

Tables Used in This Study
-------------------------
hosp/patients.csv         Patient demographics and date of death
hosp/admissions.csv       Hospital admission and discharge timestamps
hosp/diagnoses_icd.csv    ICD-10/9 diagnoses per admission (with seq_num)
hosp/d_icd_diagnoses.csv  ICD code → human-readable title lookup
hosp/prescriptions.csv    Medication prescriptions with start/stop dates
hosp/labevents.csv        Laboratory test results with timestamps
hosp/d_labitems.csv       Lab item ID → label lookup
icu/icustays.csv          ICU stay records with in/out times and LOS

Prediction Target
-----------------
Binary classification: 30-day hospital readmission.
Label = 1 if the patient is re-admitted within 30 days of discharge.
Label = 0 otherwise.
Derived entirely from admission timestamps (no external labelling needed).
